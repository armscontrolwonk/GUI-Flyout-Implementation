PBI Blanket

Name: PBI Blanket
Database: NASA Ames Thermal Protection Materials
Category: Silicon-Based Reusable Composites: Composite Flexible Blankets
Composition: Polybenzimidazole (carbon, hydrogen, oxygen, nitrogen, sulfur)
Manufacturer: Celanese
Technical Readiness Level: 6
Last Modified: 2001-10-01

Description:

  • PBI is an organic fiber that is thermally stable at relatively high temperatures, retaining most of its strength after short periods of exposure at 750 ¡F. It possesses very good resistance to most acids, bases and chemicals although it does lose some strength after exposure to UV light, typical of many high strength organic fibers. It is readily woven into felts, or fabric and can be used as thermal protection blankets, or thermal protection clothing, for example. Other uses could be as filter media for adverse operating environments that would destroy conventional materials.
