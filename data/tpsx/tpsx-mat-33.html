Carbon Aerogel

Name: Carbon Aerogel
Database: NASA Ames Thermal Protection Materials
Category: Nano-Materials
Composition: carbon
Manufacturer: Ocellus, Nanopore, Aspen Systems
Technical Readiness Level: 3
Last Modified: 2001-10-01

Description:

  • Carbon aerogels are formed by pyrolyzing an organic aerogel under an inert atmosphere. This method produces an extremely high-purity, covalently bonded highly porous carbon framework. Carbon aerogels are unique in having a high electrical conductivity. The surface area of carbon aerogels ranges from 600-930 square meters per gram.
