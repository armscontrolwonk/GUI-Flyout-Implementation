Description:

  • Advanced Carbon-Carbon (ACC) is an improved version of RCC, developed in part by LaRC, exhibiting greater strength, increased resistance to oxidation, and reduced fabrication time. The ACC composites generally consist of PAN (pol acrylonitrile) precursors rather than the rayon used for RCC. These PAN precursors can be set in a variety of available matrices. Some of these matrices include modified phenolics, acetylene terminated compound, polyimides and furanic based matrices. The ACC material is produced by weaving the carbon fibers together to produce a two dimensional structure. This fabric is then impregnated with resin and heated. After this fiber/matric system is formed, it is coated with and oxidation protection material which is most often SiC.
