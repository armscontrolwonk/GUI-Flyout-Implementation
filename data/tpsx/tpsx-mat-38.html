Description:

  • Sprayable silicone based ablator manufactured by Martin Marietta and used as the aft heatshield for the Mars Viking Missions. SLA-561S is a low density, low thermal conductivity ablator/insulator. It is available as molded panels and is sprayed on the a substrate. It is also being used as the heat shield of the back shell for Mars/Pathfinder. SLA-561 was also used for the space shuttle external tank - Tank interference heating locations, LH2 aft dome, LO2 ogive (ascent heat protection).
