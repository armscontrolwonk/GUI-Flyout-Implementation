SiC/SiC (Silicon-Carbide/Silicon-Carbide)

Name: SiC/SiC (Silicon-Carbide/Silicon-Carbide)
Database: NASA Ames Thermal Protection Materials
Category: Carbon-Based Reusable Composites: Carbon Ceramics
Composition: N/A
Manufacturer: DuPont and the French company SEP
Technical Readiness Level: N/A
Last Modified: 1998-07-17

Description:

  • Silicon-Carbide fiber reinforced Silicon-Carbide matrix is a ceramic matrix composite comprised of SiC fiber reinforcement in a SiC matrix. The SiC is used because of its excellent oxidation resistance. Use of SiC fibers gives this material somewhat better oxidation resistance than the carbon fiber reinforced material, C/SiC, but also lower strengths and stiffness at high temperatures. The SiC/SiC material is produced by depositing a SiC matrix on Silicon-Carbide fibers via Chemical Vapor Deposition (CVD). <BR><BR> Characterization of this material for TPS applications has been pursued by NASA Ames. Current producers of the material are DuPont and the French Company SEP.
