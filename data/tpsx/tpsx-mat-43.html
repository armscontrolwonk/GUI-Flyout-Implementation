PICA [virgin]

Name: PICA [virgin]
Database: NASA Ames Thermal Protection Materials
Category: Carbon-Based Ablators
Composition: Low density fibrous carbon substrate, infiltrated w/ phenolic
Manufacturer: Fiber Materials, Inc.
Technical Readiness Level: 5
Last Modified: 2001-10-01

Description:

  • The fibrous carbon substrate is manufactured by Fiber Materials Inc. (FMI) and is known as FiberForm(R), which is sold commercially for furnace liners. The FiberForm(R) is impregnated with phenolic to a final density of approximately 0.24 g/cc. The ablator PICA shows a particularly high effective heat of ablation at heat fluxes between approximately 300 - 1500 W/sqcm. PICA was selected as the forebody heatshield for the Stardust Discovery Class Mission which launched in February 1999. After collecting samples from the tail of the Wild-2 comet, Stardust will return to earth in 2006 and re-enter at 16 Km/sec; the fastest re-entry into earth's atmosphere, to date, for a man-made vehicle.
