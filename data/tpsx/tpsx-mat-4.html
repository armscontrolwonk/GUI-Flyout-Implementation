FRCI-20 Rigid Tile

Name: FRCI-20 Rigid Tile
Database: NASA Ames Thermal Protection Materials
Category: Silicon-Based Reusable Composites: Rigid Ceramic Tiles
Composition: Approximately 78% wt. silica (SiO2), 22% wt. aluminoborosilicate (62% Al2O3, 24% SiO2, 14% B2O3)
Manufacturer: Boeing, Forrest Machining, United Space Alliance (USA)
Technical Readiness Level: 5
Last Modified: 2001-10-01

Description:

  • FRCI-20 is a relatively high density (~20 lbs/cu.ft.), low thermal conductivity fibrous insulation material with higher strength than the FRCI-12 baselined on the shuttle orbiters. It is not flying on the orbiters, but it represents a significantly higher strength and higher thermal mass option as a TPS. It has been proposed to use it as a Òpicture frameÓ around blankets to protect them, but has only been used successfully on the DCX vehicle.
