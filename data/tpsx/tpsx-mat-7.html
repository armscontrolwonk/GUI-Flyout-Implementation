RCG Coating

Name: RCG Coating
Database: NASA Ames Thermal Protection Materials
Category: Coatings
Composition: 2.5% SiB4, 97.5% (94.25% B2O3, 5.75% SiO2)
Manufacturer: Boeing, United Space Alliance (USA)
Technical Readiness Level: 9
Last Modified: 2001-10-01

Description:

  • Reaction Cured Glass (RCG) is an amorphous, low expansion coefficient, high emissivity coating capable of application to high silica insulation substrates with temperature capability to about 2800 ¡F.
